Example

Private balance

Deposit once. Your tokens become private notes that only you can spend.

Private positions

Your liquidity sits in the bins with everyone else's. Size, range and earnings stay yours.

No wallet signature

A relayer sends your transaction, so your wallet never shows up next to it.

Sealed swaps

Swaps are sealed and settled together in batches. Nobody can see your order and jump ahead of it.

Proofs on your device

The maths that proves it's your money is done in your browser. Your keys never leave it.

Withdraw anywhere

Take your tokens out to any wallet, even a brand-new one, with no link back to where they came from.
