Good habits

1
Withdraw to a new walletNever back to the wallet you deposited from. Veyl blocks that, and any address with history.
2
Give it timeWait a while between depositing and withdrawing. The more people move in between, the harder you are to follow.
3
Use common amountsRound numbers blend in. An unusual amount in and the same amount out can be matched.
4
Use sealed swaps for sizeBig swaps go in a sealed batch: nobody sees your order or trades ahead of it.
5
Back up your keysYour private balance lives in this browser. An encrypted backup is the only way to recover it.
